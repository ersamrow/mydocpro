# MyDocPro

> Compress, resize and convert images, merge and split PDFs, and prepare application photos for free. Files stay on your device while you use the tools.

- [Resize an Image in CM, MM, Inches or Pixels](https://mydocpro.co.in/resize-image): Set photo dimensions in pixels, cm, mm or inches. Choose DPI and a crop or white padding, then save the resized image as JPG, PNG or WebP.
