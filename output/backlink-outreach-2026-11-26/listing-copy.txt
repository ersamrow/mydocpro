Short description:

Full description:
MyDocPro is a free browser-based toolkit for preparing images, PDFs and application photos. Resize images in pixels, centimetres, millimetres or inches; compress photos toward a target file size; crop and convert images; and use photo-size guides and a CM-to-pixels calculator.

The PDF tools support merging, splitting, organizing and compressing PDFs, as well as image-to-PDF and PDF-to-image conversion. Selected files are processed on the user's device. No account is required, and the tools do not add a watermark.

MyDocPro is designed for everyday paperwork, such as preparing form attachments, arranging scanned documents and sizing photos. Users should check downloaded files against the receiving organization's requirements.
